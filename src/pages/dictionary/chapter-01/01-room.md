## Picture words

| No. | Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [equipment rack](/dictionary/entries/equipment-rack/) | A frame for mounting equipment. |
| 2 | [equipment cabinet](/dictionary/entries/equipment-cabinet/) | An enclosure for equipment. |
| 3 | [cable tray](/dictionary/entries/cable-tray/) | A structure supporting cables along a route. |
| 4 | [uninterruptible power supply (UPS)](/dictionary/entries/ups/) | A system that supplies power during an interruption. |
| 5 | [patch panel](/dictionary/entries/patch-panel/) | An organized set of cable connection points. |

## Examples and usage

> The equipment rack is in the telecommunications room.

> The cables run along the overhead cable tray.

> The switch is mounted below the patch panel.

> The UPS supplies power during a power interruption.

## Room names

**[telecommunications room](/dictionary/entries/telecommunications-room/)**: a room housing cable terminations and associated equipment.

**[main distribution frame (MDF)](/dictionary/entries/mdf/)**: a primary distribution frame. The term *MDF* is also used informally for the room housing it.

**[intermediate distribution frame (IDF)](/dictionary/entries/idf/)**: an intermediate distribution frame. In site conversations, *IDF* can also mean its room.

> The access switches are in the third-floor IDF room.

> Could you arrange access to the MDF room for the site survey?
