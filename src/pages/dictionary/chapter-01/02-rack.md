# 02 · A closer look at the rack

**CHAPTER 01 · VISUAL VOCABULARY**

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [Picture guide](/dictionary/#visual-pages) · [English index](/dictionary/)

Follow the numbered parts of a rack and its cable connections.

![02 · A closer look at the rack: numbered illustration; names and definitions follow.](/dictionary/images/chapter01-rack-numbered.svg)

*The picture is illustrative; equipment and layouts vary by site.*

## Picture words

| No. | Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [rack rail](/dictionary/entries/rack-rail/) | A mounting rail that helps secure equipment. |
| 2 | [patch panel](/dictionary/entries/patch-panel/) | A panel of cable connection points. |
| 3 | [network switch](/dictionary/entries/network-switch/) | A device that forwards traffic between its connections. |
| 4 | [cable manager](/dictionary/entries/cable-manager/) | A guide that organizes cables. |
| 5 | [power distribution unit (PDU)](/dictionary/entries/pdu/) | A unit that distributes power to connected equipment. |
| 6 | [patch cord](/dictionary/entries/patch-cord/) | A short cable connecting equipment or connection points. |

## Examples and usage

> Mount the switch on the rack rails.

> Connect the patch cord to port 12 on the patch panel.

> Route the patch cords through the cable manager.

> Check the label on the switch port.

## Position and space

**[rack unit (U)](/dictionary/entries/rack-unit/)**: a unit of mounting height. One U equals 1.75 inches (44.45 mm).

**[rack elevation](/dictionary/entries/rack-elevation/)**: a drawing showing the positions of devices in a rack.

**[rack location](/dictionary/entries/rack-location/)**: the recorded site, room, rack and mounting position.

**[blanking panel](/dictionary/entries/blanking-panel/)**: a panel covering an unused rack opening.

**[switch port](/dictionary/entries/switch-port/)**: a physical interface for a cable or transceiver.

> The 2U device is installed at U18 in rack R02.

> Please update the rack elevation after the installation.
