## Picture words

| No. | Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [cold aisle](/dictionary/entries/cold-aisle/) | An aisle supplying cool air to equipment intakes. |
| 2 | [air intake](/dictionary/entries/air-intake/) | An opening through which air enters equipment. |
| 3 | [exhaust vent](/dictionary/entries/exhaust-vent/) | An opening through which air leaves equipment. |
| 4 | [hot aisle](/dictionary/entries/hot-aisle/) | An aisle receiving warm exhaust air. |
| 5 | [perforated floor tile](/dictionary/entries/perforated-floor-tile/) | A floor tile with openings that let air pass through. |
| 6 | [raised floor](/dictionary/entries/raised-floor/) | A floor with a space beneath the walking surface. |

## Examples and usage

> Cool air enters through the air intake.

> Warm air leaves through the exhaust vent.

> Keep the airflow path clear.

> Check the temperature near the equipment intake.

## Cooling terms

**[airflow](/dictionary/entries/airflow/)**: the movement of air through equipment or a space.

**[room air conditioner](/dictionary/entries/room-air-conditioner/)**: equipment that cools and conditions the air in a room.

**[temperature sensor](/dictionary/entries/temperature-sensor/)**: a device that measures temperature at a specific location.

> The temperature sensor reports a high reading.

> The room air conditioner is operating normally.

**Usage note:** This image shows one simplified arrangement. Actual device intake and exhaust directions vary by model; not all rooms use a raised floor or hot/cold aisles.
