## Say it at work

**Arranging access**

> Could you arrange access to the telecommunications room for the site survey?

> Please confirm the access window and whether an escort is required.

**Finding a device**

> Could you confirm the room, rack ID and U position of the switch?

> It is in IDF-03, rack R02, at U18.

**Checking cables and power**

> Please trace this cable to the patch panel and verify the port label.

> Are both power supplies connected to independent power sources?

**Reporting a condition**

> The room temperature is above the agreed threshold.

> The UPS reports an alarm. I will notify the facilities team.

## Quick review

1. What is the difference between a rack and a cabinet?
2. Which component organizes cable connection points but does not switch Ethernet frames?
3. What do 2U and U18 each describe?
4. What does a UPS do, and what does a PDU do?
5. Why should you verify the direction of airflow for a specific device?
6. Ask a colleague for the exact location of a switch.

**Answers**

1. A rack is a mounting frame; a cabinet is an enclosure.
2. A patch panel.
3. 2U is a height; U18 is a mounting position.
4. A UPS can supply stored energy during an interruption; a PDU distributes power.
5. Intake and exhaust directions vary by device and configuration.
6. “Could you confirm the room, rack ID and U position of the switch?”
