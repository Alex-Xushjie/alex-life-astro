# rack elevation

**Countable noun.** · Chapter 01 · `technical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

## Definition

A diagram showing the vertical positions of equipment within a rack.

## Usage and distinctions

A rack elevation is a drawing of vertical equipment positions. Label rack-unit numbering and distinguish a planned layout from an as-built record.

## Common combinations

update the rack elevation; an as-built rack elevation; U position

## Examples

> Update the rack elevation after moving the firewall to U22.

> The as-built rack elevation shows the switch in R03 at U18.
