# power supply module

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

[See this term in the 03 power visual spread](/dictionary/chapter-01/03-power/).

## Definition

A removable or integrated component that supplies electrical power to a device.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. Some devices support more than one module; redundancy depends on the device, available capacity, and power sources.

## Common combinations

replace a power supply module; redundant power supplies; power supply status

## Examples

> The switch has two power supply modules.

> Check the status of both power supplies.
