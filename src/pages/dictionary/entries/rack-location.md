# rack location

**Technical term** · Chapter 01 · `technical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

## Definition

The recorded position of an item within a site, room, rack, and mounting position.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. A rack ID and U position describe location; a height such as 2U describes the amount of vertical space occupied.

## Common combinations

rack ID; U position; equipment location

## Examples

> The rack location is IDF-03, R02, U18.

> Please confirm the rack location in the asset record.
