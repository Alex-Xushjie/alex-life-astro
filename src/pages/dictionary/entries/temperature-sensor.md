# temperature sensor

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

[See this term in the 04 cooling visual spread](/dictionary/chapter-01/04-cooling/).

## Definition

A device that measures temperature at a particular location.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. A sensor reading represents its location; room temperature and equipment intake temperature can differ.

## Common combinations

temperature reading; intake sensor; environmental monitoring

## Examples

> The temperature sensor reports a high reading.

> Check the temperature at the equipment intake.
