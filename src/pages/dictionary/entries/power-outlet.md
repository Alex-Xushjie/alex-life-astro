# power outlet

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

[See this term in the 03 power visual spread](/dictionary/chapter-01/03-power/).

## Definition

A socket that provides an electrical connection for a plug.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. PDU outlets and wall outlets can have different connector types and ratings.

## Common combinations

PDU outlet; outlet label; available outlet

## Examples

> Connect the power cord to the labeled PDU outlet.

> The PDU has one unused outlet.
