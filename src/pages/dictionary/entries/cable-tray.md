# cable tray

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

[See this term in the 01 room visual spread](/dictionary/chapter-01/01-room/).

## Definition

A supporting structure that carries and organizes cables along a route.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. A tray supports cables; it does not by itself identify their destinations.

## Common combinations

overhead cable tray; route cables; cable pathway

## Examples

> The fiber cables run along the overhead cable tray.

> Check the cable tray before adding another bundle.
