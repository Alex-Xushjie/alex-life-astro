# cold aisle

**Countable noun.** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

## Definition

An aisle that supplies cool air to equipment intakes in a hot-aisle/cold-aisle layout.

## Usage and distinctions

The cold aisle supplies intake air. Check device intake temperatures and the cooling design rather than assuming a universal temperature limit.

## Common combinations

cold-aisle containment; intake air; cold-air supply

## Examples

> The switch intakes face the cold aisle.

> Check the intake temperature rather than relying only on the room thermostat.
