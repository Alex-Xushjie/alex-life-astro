# rack unit

**Countable noun; abbreviations: U and RU.** · Chapter 01 · `technical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

## Definition

A unit of vertical mounting height for rack equipment; one rack unit is 1.75 inches (44.45 mm).

## Usage and distinctions

One rack unit is 1.75 inches (44.45 mm) of nominal mounting height. 2U describes height; U20 identifies a position. Neither specifies cabinet depth.

## Common combinations

a 1U switch; two rack units; occupy 2U of rack space

## Examples

> The appliance occupies 2U of rack space.

> Reserve 4U for the planned expansion.
