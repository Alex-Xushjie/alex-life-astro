# room air conditioner

**Countable noun** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

[See this term in the 04 cooling visual spread](/dictionary/chapter-01/04-cooling/).

## Definition

Equipment that cools and conditions the air in a room.

## Usage and distinctions

Countable noun. Data-center cooling systems use different architectures; do not assume that every site has the same type of unit.

## Common combinations

air-conditioning unit; room cooling; cooling capacity

## Examples

> The room air conditioner is operating normally.

> Report the cooling alarm to the facilities team.
