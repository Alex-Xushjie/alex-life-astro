# equipment cabinet

**Countable noun.** · Chapter 01 · `physical`

[Chapter](/dictionary/) · [English index](/dictionary/)

![Illustrated detail: equipment cabinet.](/dictionary/images/visual-equipment-cabinet.svg)

*An enclosure with doors and mounting rails. This is an illustrative detail; actual equipment varies.*

## Definition

An enclosure used to house equipment, often with mounting rails, doors, and side panels.

## Usage and distinctions

A cabinet usually has an enclosure, doors, and mounting rails. Check depth, weight capacity, access, and airflow when selecting one.

## Common combinations

a locked cabinet; cabinet depth; front and rear doors

## Examples

> The cabinet is deep enough for the switch and its rear cable clearance.

> Keep the cabinet doors closed as required by the cooling design.
